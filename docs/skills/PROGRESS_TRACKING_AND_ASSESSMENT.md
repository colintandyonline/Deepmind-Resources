# Progress Tracking and Assessment Skill

This skill defines how DeepMindResources should measure user progress, practical skill gain, sandbox completion, assessments, confidence, business team training, and next-step recommendations.

Progress tracking should prove learning, not just count clicks.

---

## Core Purpose

DeepMindResources exists to help users gain practical AI skill.

The platform promise is:

AI update → verified meaning → practical lesson → sandbox task → user skill gain

Progress tracking is how the platform proves that final step.

---

## Core Rule

Do not treat reading a page as the same as learning a skill.

A user has made real progress when they can understand, apply, practise, compare, validate, or explain something better than before.

Progress should measure skill development, not just content consumption.

---

## Progress Types

DeepMindResources may track:

- Lesson progress
- Learning path progress
- Sandbox task progress
- Skill confidence
- Assessment results
- Tool comparison practice
- AI validation ability
- Business workflow practice
- Team training progress
- Manager review status
- Next recommended step

---

## Lesson Progress

Lesson progress may include:

- Not started
- In progress
- Completed
- Needs review
- Updated since completion
- Related sandbox task pending

Lesson completion should not always mean skill mastery.

Where possible, lessons should connect to a practice task or assessment.

---

## Sandbox Progress

Sandbox progress should carry more skill weight than reading progress.

Sandbox task statuses may include:

- Not started
- Started
- Submitted
- Passed
- Needs improvement
- Reviewed
- Completed

A sandbox task should have success criteria so completion means something.

---

## Skill Areas

Progress should map to skill areas such as:

- AI fundamentals
- Prompt engineering
- Tool selection
- AI for business productivity
- Tool calling and functions
- AI agents
- RAG and knowledge systems
- Agentic workflows
- Multi-agent systems
- AI safety and hallucination control
- Source validation
- Business automation
- AI research workflows
- AI coding workflows

Skill-level progress is more useful than only tracking individual pages.

---

## Assessment Types

Assessments may include:

- Quick checks
- Reflection questions
- Practical tasks
- Scenario questions
- Sandbox submissions
- Model comparison reviews
- Tool selection exercises
- Source validation challenges
- Business workflow plans
- Manager-reviewed team tasks

Assessments should match the skill being taught.

---

## Confidence Tracking

Users may self-rate confidence before or after lessons and tasks.

Useful confidence levels:

- Not confident yet
- Understands the basics
- Can use with guidance
- Can apply independently
- Can explain to others
- Can build or manage workflows

Confidence should be treated as one signal, not proof by itself.

---

## Evidence of Skill

Good evidence of skill may include:

- Completed sandbox task
- Correctly improved a prompt
- Compared two AI tools for a real scenario
- Identified hallucinations or weak sources
- Built a simple agent workflow
- Completed a RAG retrieval task
- Designed a business AI workflow
- Passed a validation challenge
- Explained trade-offs clearly

Progress should prioritise practical evidence over passive reading.

---

## Next-Step Recommendations

Progress tracking should help recommend what the user should do next.

Recommendations may be based on:

- Current learning path
- Completed lessons
- Missed assessments
- Sandbox results
- Confidence score
- Skill gaps
- New AI updates affecting a completed lesson
- Business role or team pathway

A useful progress system should guide users forward.

---

## Updated Content Rules

Because AI changes quickly, completed lessons may become outdated.

When a lesson changes due to a verified update, progress should support statuses such as:

- Completed before update
- Refresher recommended
- Updated lesson available
- New sandbox task added
- Skill recheck recommended

This helps DeepMindResources stay alive rather than static.

---

## Business Team Progress

Business progress tracking should support teams and managers.

Possible business progress fields:

- Team member
- Role
- Assigned learning path
- Lesson completion
- Sandbox completion
- Skill confidence
- Manager review
- Practical workflow evidence
- Risk/safety training status
- Next recommended module

Business dashboards should show skill readiness, not just logins.

---

## Manager and Team Reporting

Manager-facing reports may include:

- Team completion summary
- Skill gap summary
- Role-based progress
- Sandbox task results
- Staff confidence trends
- AI safety training status
- Workflow implementation readiness
- Recommended next training

Reports should be simple and actionable.

---

## Progress Data Rules

Progress records should include:

- User ID
- Lesson ID where relevant
- Sandbox task ID where relevant
- Learning path ID where relevant
- Skill area
- Status
- Completion date
- Assessment result where relevant
- Confidence rating where relevant
- Evidence of completion where relevant
- Next recommended step

Do not store unnecessary sensitive data.

---

## Assessment Quality Rules

Assessments should be:

- Clear
- Relevant
- Practical
- Fair for the difficulty level
- Connected to learning outcomes
- Easy to review
- Useful for next-step recommendations

Avoid assessments that only check memorisation when the skill is practical.

---

## Business Assessment Rules

Business assessments should focus on workplace readiness.

They may test whether staff can:

- Use AI safely
- Choose suitable tools
- Avoid sharing sensitive data
- Validate AI output
- Apply prompts to real tasks
- Follow company policy
- Design simple workflow improvements

Business assessments should avoid unnecessary technical detail unless the role requires it.

---

## Public and Private Progress

Progress is user-specific and should be private by default.

Team progress should only be visible to approved team admins or managers.

Do not expose personal learning data publicly.

---

## Agent Responsibilities

### Lesson Builder Agent

Defines lesson learning outcomes and quick checks.

### Sandbox Builder Agent

Defines practical success criteria.

### Business Training Agent

Defines role-based progress and team readiness signals.

### Master Brain Workflow

Flags when completed content has changed due to new verified updates.

### Validator Agent

Ensures assessments are based on accurate, current information.

---

## Definition of Done

A progress or assessment feature is complete when it tracks:

- What the user studied
- What skill area it belongs to
- Whether the user practised the skill
- Whether there is evidence of skill gain
- Confidence or assessment result where useful
- Whether content has changed since completion
- The next recommended step

---

## Locked Rule

DeepMindResources should measure practical AI skill gain, not just content completion.

Progress tracking should help users and teams know what they can actually do next.
