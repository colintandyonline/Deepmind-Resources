# Business Training Skill

This skill defines how DeepMindResources should create business-facing AI training, team learning paths, staff guidance, and practical workplace AI adoption material.

Business AI training is one of the strongest commercial angles for DeepMindResources.

---

## Core Purpose

The Business Training skill helps organisations use AI tools safely, practically, and effectively.

It should turn verified AI intelligence into workplace-ready training for teams, staff, managers, and business owners.

The goal is not to sell hype.

The goal is to help businesses reduce wasted AI spend, improve staff capability, and apply AI tools to real workflows.

---

## Product Promise Connection

Business training should support the DeepMindResources product loop:

AI update → verified meaning → practical lesson → sandbox task → user skill gain

For businesses, this becomes:

AI update → verified business meaning → staff training → workflow practice → team capability gain

## Required Business Training Questions

Before creating business training material, answer:

1. Which business role or team is this for?
2. What workplace problem does it solve?
3. What AI skill does it teach?
4. What tool or workflow is involved?
5. Is the tool suitable for non-technical staff?
6. What risks or limitations matter?
7. What privacy or data concerns exist?
8. What level of training is required?
9. What outcome should the business expect?
10. How can the skill be practised?
11. How should progress be measured?
12. Is the recommendation based on verified information?
13. Does this affect tool spend or subscription choices?
14. Does this need a team dashboard or progress record?

## Training Levels

### Awareness Level

For staff who need to understand what AI can and cannot do.

Should cover:

- Basic AI concepts
- Safe use
- Hallucination awareness
- Company rules
- When not to use AI

### Practical User Level

For staff who will use AI in daily work.

Should cover:

- Prompting
- Workflow examples
- Tool choice
- Checking outputs
- Productivity habits

### Power User Level

For staff who build repeatable workflows.

Should cover:

- Automation
- Tool chaining
- Workflow design
- Validation
- Templates and reusable processes

### Technical Builder Level

For staff building systems or integrations.

Should cover:

- APIs
- RAG
- Agents
- Tool calling
- Security and data handling
- Deployment considerations

### Manager Level

For leaders responsible for adoption.

Should cover:

- Tool selection
- Staff training plans
- Risk management
- Progress tracking
- Cost control
- Workflow prioritisation

## Business Training Quality Rules

Business material must be:

- Practical
- Role-aware
- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Current
- Risk-aware
- Tool-agnostic unless a tool is clearly justified
- Focused on real workflows
- Suitable for the target skill level

Avoid:

- Unrealistic productivity promises
- Hype-driven tool recommendations
- Overly technical content for non-technical teams
- Generic business buzzwords
- Advice that ignores privacy or security
- Recommendations without cost or plan awareness

---

## Tool Recommendation Rules for Business

Business tool recommendations should consider:

- Use case fit
- Staff skill level
- Pricing and plan limits
- Team/admin controls
- Security and privacy
- Data handling
- Integrations
- Support and documentation
- Reliability
- Training burden
- Total cost of ownership
- Risk of vendor lock-in

Do not recommend a tool to a business just because it is popular or newly launched.

---

## Privacy and Security Rules

Business training should teach safe AI use.

It should warn staff not to paste sensitive information into tools unless approved.

Sensitive information may include:

- Customer data
- Employee data
- Financial records
- Legal documents
- Medical information
- Private company strategy
- Credentials or secrets
- Proprietary code
- Confidential contracts

Business training should use synthetic examples unless safe data handling is clearly established.

## Workflow Training Rules

Business workflow lessons should explain:

- Current manual process
- AI-assisted process
- Tools involved
- Staff roles
- Input data
- Output expected
- Review step
- Risk controls
- Success metric

Do not automate broken processes without understanding the business workflow.

- Role-based pathway progress
- Manager review
- Workflow implementation evidence
- Confidence rating
- Practical output review

## Business Sandbox Tasks

Business training should link to practical sandbox tasks where useful.

Examples:

- Write and improve a customer support prompt.
- Compare two tools for a staff workflow.
- Build a simple document-summary checklist.
- Design a safe internal knowledge workflow.
- Create a meeting-summary validation checklist.
- Simulate an automation approval process.
- Identify risky AI usage in sample scenarios.

Business sandbox tasks should be safe, practical, and role-aware.

---

## Verified Update Briefings

When major AI updates happen, DeepMindResources may create business briefings.

A business briefing should answer:

- What changed?
- Is it verified?
- Which teams are affected?
- Does this change tool recommendations?
- Does this require staff retraining?
- Does this affect costs?
- Does this create privacy, security, or compliance concerns?
- What should managers do next?
