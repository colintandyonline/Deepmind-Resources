# SEO and AI Search Skill

This skill defines how DeepMindResources should build public-facing pages so they are useful to humans, understandable to Google and Bing, and readable by AI search and answer systems.

This file expands the SEO and AI-search rules from `docs/SKILLS.md` into a dedicated operating standard.

---

## Core Purpose

DeepMindResources should be discoverable by people searching for practical AI training, AI tool comparisons, agentic workflows, RAG, prompt engineering, business AI training, and current AI intelligence.

SEO is not just keyword placement.

For DeepMindResources, SEO means clear page purpose, useful content, trustworthy structure, and strong search intent alignment.

AI search optimisation means making content easy for LLM-based search systems to understand, summarise, cite, and classify correctly.

---

## Core Rule

Every public page must have:

- Clear search intent
- Unique metadata
- One clear H1
- Logical heading structure
- Plain-English summary near the top
- Useful human-readable content
- LLM-readable definitions and explanations
- Internal links where useful
- No placeholder metadata
- No generic v0 copy
- No unsupported AI hype

---

## Page Metadata Requirements

Every public route should define:

- Page title
- Meta description
- Open Graph title
- Open Graph description
- Canonical URL where applicable
- Social preview text where applicable
- Page intent note in planning or content docs

Avoid defaults such as:

- `v0 App`
- `Created with v0`
- `Dashboard`
- `Untitled`
- Duplicate page descriptions

---

## Title Rules

A good title should be specific and useful.

It should usually include:

- Main topic
- Audience or use case where useful
- DeepMindResources brand where appropriate

Examples:

- AI Tool Selection for Business Teams | DeepMindResources
- Prompt Engineering Lessons for Practical AI Workflows
- Agentic AI Systems Training and Sandbox Practice
- Best AI Tools for Research, Coding, and Business Automation

Avoid:

- Home
- Dashboard
- AI Page
- Best AI Ever
- Ultimate AI Tool Ranking

---

## Meta Description Rules

Meta descriptions should explain the page value clearly.

They should usually answer:

- What is the page about?
- Who is it for?
- What can the user learn or do?
- Why is it trustworthy or practical?

Good descriptions should be natural, not stuffed with repeated keywords.

---

## Search Intent Types

Each page should be planned around a search intent.

Common DeepMindResources intents:

- Learn AI fundamentals
- Learn prompt engineering
- Compare AI tools
- Choose an AI tool for a task
- Learn AI agents
- Learn tool calling
- Learn RAG
- Practise AI workflows
- Understand AI updates
- Train staff on AI
- Reduce wasted AI tool spend
- Build agentic workflows
- Validate AI outputs

---

## LLM-Readable Structure

AI search systems need clear structure.

Pages should include:

- Direct summaries
- Clear definitions
- Short explanatory paragraphs
- Tables where comparison matters
- FAQ sections where useful
- Practical examples
- Specific use cases
- Named audience groups
- Clear next steps

Avoid hiding the meaning of a page behind vague branding language.

---

## Recommended Public Page Pattern

A strong public page should usually follow this pattern:

1. H1 with clear page topic
2. Short summary explaining what the page does
3. Who this page is for
4. Why the topic matters
5. Main content sections
6. Practical examples or workflows
7. Related lessons or sandbox tasks
8. Trust or validation note where relevant
9. FAQ section where useful
10. Clear CTA

---

## AI Search Positioning

DeepMindResources should consistently communicate:

DeepMindResources is a living AI training and tool-intelligence platform that turns verified AI updates into practical lessons, sandbox tasks, and business-ready learning paths.

This meaning should appear naturally across the site.

Do not repeat the exact sentence mechanically on every page.

---

## Comparison Page SEO

Tool comparison pages should target scenario-based intent.

Good comparison intents:

- Best AI tool for coding
- Best AI tool for research
- Best AI tool for business automation
- ChatGPT vs Claude for writing
- n8n vs Zapier for AI workflows
- Cursor vs Replit for AI development

Comparison pages should include:

- Short answer
- Comparison table
- Best by scenario
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Pricing or plan caveats
- Trust and evidence notes
- Related lessons and sandbox tasks

---

## Lesson Page SEO

Lesson pages should target learning intent.

They should include:

- What the learner will learn
- Skill level
- Clear explanation
- Practical example
- Common mistakes
- Practice task
- Related lessons

Lesson pages should not read like thin blog posts.

They should teach something useful.

---

## Business Page SEO

Business pages should target practical workplace intent.

They should include:

- Business problem
- Audience or team type
- Workflow value
- Tool considerations
- Risk and privacy notes
- Training recommendation
- Staff practice tasks
- Clear next step

Avoid vague claims such as “transform your business with AI”.

---

## Technical SEO Checklist

Before a public page is complete, check:

- Unique title
- Unique meta description
- Correct H1
- Sensible H2/H3 structure
- Mobile layout works
- Page loads without layout squeeze
- No placeholder metadata
- No placeholder copy
- Clear first-screen purpose
- Internal links where useful
- CTA exists
- Content supports the product promise
- AI search systems can understand the page topic

---

## Structured Data Future Rules

When pages stabilise, add structured data where appropriate.

Possible schema types:

- Organization
- WebSite
- WebApplication
- Course
- FAQPage
- BreadcrumbList
- Article
- SoftwareApplication
- Dataset, where relevant to verified AI intelligence records

Structured data must describe real visible content.

Do not add fake schema.

---

## Internal Linking Rules

Public pages should connect the platform together.

Good internal links include:

- Lessons to sandbox tasks
- Tool comparison pages to tool selection lessons
- AI update pages to affected lessons
- Business pages to relevant training paths
- Agent pages to tool-calling and RAG lessons

Internal links should help users move from reading to practising.

---

## Content Quality Rules

Search-focused content must still be useful.

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing
- Thin pages
- Unsupported rankings
- Rewritten generic AI content
- Repeated intros across pages
- Fake authority
- Pages with no practical next step

Every page should help the user make progress.

---

## Definition of Done

A page is SEO and AI-search ready when it has:

- Defined search intent
- Unique metadata
- Clear page structure
- Useful summary
- Human-readable content
- LLM-readable definitions and headings
- Internal links
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Trustworthy claims
- Clear next step

---

## Locked Rule

DeepMindResources should be optimised for humans first, search engines second, and AI search systems third.

Good structure serves all three.
